Profile

I am a molecular plant biologist with a strong background in both experimental and computational plant science. My research focuses on plant–microbe interactions, molecular immune responses, and functional genomics of disease resistance, especially in the context of Fusarium wilt TR4 in banana.

I work extensively with molecular and plant biology techniques including gel electrophoresis, RT-PCR, 3′ RACE, molecular cloning, real-time PCR (qPCR), transient tobacco expression, virus-induced gene silencing (VIGS), plant crossing, hydroponics cultivation, pathogen inoculation, and advanced microscopy including upright, inverted, and confocal systems.

Bioinformatics & Computational Skills

On the bioinformatics side, I perform SNP calling from RNA-seq and whole-genome sequencing data, transcriptome differential expression analysis, and WGS coverage profiling. My workflow includes scripting and data processing in R, Linux, and Python.

I also work regularly with Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Benchling, Primer3, MEGA 11, ModelTest-NG, GraphPad Prism, SnapGene, Vector NTI, EndNote, and Zen microscopy systems.
