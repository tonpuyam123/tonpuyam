About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in molecular plant biology, with research interests in plant pathology, molecular breeding, functional genomics, and host–pathogen interactions. My work focuses on understanding resistance mechanisms against Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. cubense tropical race 4 (TR4), a destructive pathogen threatening global banana production.

My recent research investigates Cavendish banana somaclonal variants with enhanced resistance to Fusarium wilt. This work integrates genomic, transcriptomic, and functional analyses to identify chromosomal deletions, susceptibility-related genes, and salicylic acid (SA)–mediated immune responses that contribute to disease resistance.

I completed my Ph.D. training in Biotechnology at National Taiwan University, conducting my research at the Agricultural Biotechnology Research Center (ABRC), Academia Sinica. Previously, I earned my M.Sc. in Genetics and Plant Breeding from the College of Post Graduate Studies in Agricultural Sciences, Central Agricultural University, Meghalaya, India, and my B.Sc. in Agriculture from Assam Agricultural University, Jorhat, India.
